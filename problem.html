😔 Why This Website Exists: Seenu's Story

In Memory of Srinivas

This initiative was born from personal tragedy. Seenu, a beloved brother and son, lost his battle with cancer not because it was untreatable, but because treatment came too late.

His journey highlighted critical gaps in our healthcare system:

  • Delayed recognition of early symptoms
  • Lack of awareness about early symptoms
  • Confusion about where to go and whom to consult
  • Financial barriers preventing timely treatment
  • System navigation challenges for patients

Seenu's passing taught us that information at the right time can be as crucial as medicine itself.

🎯 Root Causes of Treatment Delay

Awareness Gap

Problem: Don't recognize early symptoms or think "it won't happen to me"

Solution: Public education on warning signs

Navigation Challenge

Problem: Don't know which doctor to see or tests to take

Solution: Clear treatment pathway guidance

Financial Barrier

Problem: Delay treatment due to cost concerns

Solution: Information on government schemes & assistance

Access Issues

Problem: No good hospitals nearby or long waiting lists

Solution: Directory of verified cancer centers
