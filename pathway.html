🗺️ Cancer Treatment Pathway

A step-by-step guide from suspicion to survivorship. Follow these steps to ensure timely and proper treatment.

⏱️ Time is Critical

Every day matters in cancer treatment. This pathway helps you navigate the system efficiently.

Start Your Journey

📋 Your Treatment Journey

Follow these 7 steps in sequence for optimal treatment:

1
Within 48 hours

Recognition & Consultation

Action: If you notice persistent symptoms, consult a general physician immediately.

What to do:

  • Note down all symptoms and their duration
  • Collect family medical history
  • Consult a doctor within 48 hours of noticing symptoms
  • Don't ignore or delay - early action saves lives
2
Within 1 week

Initial Tests & Diagnosis

Action: Complete recommended tests to confirm or rule out cancer.

Common tests:

  • Blood tests (CBC, tumor markers)
  • Imaging (Ultrasound, X-ray, CT scan)
  • Biopsy (if suspicious growth found)
  • Pathology report analysis
3
Within 2 weeks

Specialist Consultation

Action: Consult an oncologist (cancer specialist) with your reports.

Important:

  • Find a qualified oncologist near you
  • Carry all test reports
  • Ask about cancer type, stage, and grade
  • Understand treatment options available
4
Within 3 weeks

Treatment Planning

Action: Develop a comprehensive treatment plan with your oncologist.

Treatment options:

  • Surgery (to remove tumor)
  • Chemotherapy (medication)
  • Radiation therapy
  • Targeted therapy/Immunotherapy
  • Combination of above
5
Ongoing

Treatment Phase

Action: Begin and continue treatment as planned.

During treatment:

  • Follow medication schedule strictly
  • Attend all therapy sessions
  • Manage side effects with doctor's guidance
  • Maintain nutrition and hydration
  • Keep family/caregivers involved
6
Post-treatment

Follow-up & Monitoring

Action: Regular check-ups to monitor recovery and detect recurrence.

Follow-up schedule:

  • Regular doctor visits (every 3-6 months initially)
  • Periodic scans and blood tests
  • Monitor for any new symptoms
  • Rehabilitation and support services
7
Long-term

Survivorship & Prevention

Action: Maintain healthy lifestyle and regular screenings.

Healthy practices:

  • Regular exercise and balanced diet
  • Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol
  • Manage stress and mental health
  • Annual health check-ups
  • Support other patients (share your story)
