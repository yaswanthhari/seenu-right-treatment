💰 Financial Assistance for Cancer Treatment

Treatment shouldn't be delayed due to financial constraints. Explore government schemes, NGO support, and crowdfunding options.

Who Can Apply?

Most schemes are available for:

  • Families below poverty line (BPL card holders)
  • Annual income less than ₹3-5 lakhs
  • All Indian citizens (some schemes for specific states)
  • Patients without adequate insurance coverage

🏛️ Government Health Schemes

Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY)

Coverage: ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

What's Covered:

  • All cancer treatments including chemotherapy, radiation, surgery
  • Pre and post hospitalization expenses
  • Diagnostic tests and medicines
  • No age limit or pre-existing condition exclusion

How to Apply:

1
Check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in or call 14555
2
Visit nearest Common Service Center (CSC) or empaneled hospital
3
Submit Aadhaar card, income certificate, and BPL card (if applicable)
4
Get e-card for cashless treatment at empaneled hospitals
Visit PMJAY Website

🤝 NGO & Charity Support

Cancer Patients Aid Association (CPAA)

Support Provided: Financial aid for treatment, medicines, and palliative care.

Documents Required:

1
Cancer diagnosis confirmation from government hospital
2
Income certificate and BPL card (if available)
3
Treatment cost estimate from hospital
4
Aadhaar card and recent photograph

Contact: 022-24924000 | Website: cpaaindia.org
