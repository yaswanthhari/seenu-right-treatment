⚠️ Warning Signs Checklist

Check any symptoms you have experienced for more than 2 weeks:

Unexplained weight loss (more than 5kg in a month)

Persistent fatigue not relieved by rest

Unusual lumps or swelling anywhere in body

Changes in bowel or bladder habits

Persistent cough or hoarseness

Difficulty swallowing

Unexplained bleeding or bruising

Non-healing sores or ulcers

Changes in skin moles (size, color, shape)

Persistent indigestion or discomfort after eating

❗ If you checked 3 or more items, consult a doctor within 1 week.

👨‍⚕️ For Men

Testicular lumps or swelling

Changes in urinary habits (frequency, flow)

Blood in urine or semen

👩‍⚕️ For Women

Breast lumps or thickening

Nipple changes or discharge

Abnormal vaginal bleeding

Pelvic pain or pressure
